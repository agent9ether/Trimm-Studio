Privacy

Trimm Studio, by 24HourLifestyle LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

Trimm Studio is built so your footage stays on your computer. This page lists what stays, what can leave, and who receives it when it does.

In short

  • No account, no tracking. Trimm has no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. It does not send usage data to us.
  • Your videos are processed on your computer. Importing, analysis, editing and rendering all happen locally.
  • Data leaves only when you choose a service that needs it, and only to that service. Each case is listed below.
  • Your API keys are kept in the Windows credential store and are never written to settings, logs or exports.

What stays on your computer

  • Your videos, clips, exports, transcripts, projects and settings.
  • Your API keys and connected-account tokens (in the operating system's credential store).
  • Logs and diagnostics. Logs hide keys and tokens automatically and are never sent anywhere. A diagnostics file is created only if you export one, and you decide whether to share it.

Trimm stores its data in a folder on your computer. Settings, then Storage shows where it is. In portable mode everything lives in the ClipForgeData folder next to the app.

Local-only mode (Settings, then Privacy) blocks cloud AI providers and cloud voices, add-on and voice downloads, channel checks and update checks. Two things still reach the internet because that is their purpose: importing a link contacts the site that hosts the video, and publishing contacts the platform you chose. The speech model for offline transcription downloads once on first use, so run a first transcription before you rely on it fully offline.

TikTok

If you connect TikTok, Trimm asks TikTok for your public profile (display name, avatar and account ID), your account totals (followers, following, likes and video count) and permission to upload videos or post for you.

  • It is used only to show which account is connected, to show those totals on the Analytics screen, and to send the clips you choose, with the settings you choose.
  • It stays on your computer. The sign-in tokens are kept in the operating system's credential store. Trimm has no server, so we never receive this data, and we do not sell it, share it or use it for advertising.
  • To disconnect, remove the connection in Trimm (this deletes the tokens from your computer) and revoke access in TikTok under the app's permission settings.
  • TikTok's own terms and privacy policy apply to what you post there.

AI providers have their own policies

When you use a provider, its privacy policy and terms apply to what you send it, including how long it keeps it and whether it uses it to train models. We do not control that. Read your provider's policy, and use a local model if you need the content to stay private.

Browser data

The Browse window is a normal web view. Websites you open there can set cookies and store data, and they stay in Trimm's own profile folder, separate from your other browsers. Clear them by deleting that folder.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Trimm removes the program. Your projects and settings are in the data folder, which you can delete yourself. API keys can be removed in AI & Models before you uninstall, or in the Windows credential store.

Children

Trimm Studio is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. It collects no personal information at all.

Contact and changes

Use this repository's Issues page for privacy questions. Do not post keys, passwords or private videos there. We may update this page. The date at the top shows the latest version.
